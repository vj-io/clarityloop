# ClarityLoop — demo video (2:14)

Team **Kaching** · Vaibhav Jangid · Nexconn Build Challenge 2026 · Track: Workplace & Collaboration

File: `ClarityLoop-demo-v2.mp4` (1080p, 60 fps, 70 MB; poster `ClarityLoop-demo-v2-poster.png`). Recorded on the deployed app, https://clarityloop-theta.vercel.app, from `/judge` (Ravi, Murugan, Lakshmi). Every card, translation and number on screen comes from the live app; waits are cut and typing is sped up, and a badge on screen says so. Captions and music, no voiceover.

## Beats

1. **Hook.** "Read is not understood." An instruction can be read, translated and still misunderstood.
2. **Chat in your language.** Ravi writes in English; Murugan reads Hindi and replies in Hinglish; Ravi reads English. "See original".
3. **Meaning check.** Ravi sends "Unload · cement bags · Gate 3 · 14:00 · 40 bags". There is no OK button; Murugan says it back with Gate 2. The red card shows Gate 3 vs Gate 2 while the rest matches, and Confirm is blocked. AI only reads the reply; plain code compares.
4. **Fix and understood.** Same chat: Ravi corrects, Murugan restates, the card turns green, both confirm, Understood.
5. **Crew task and roll-call.** One task to the whole crew, in Hindi and Tamil. The roll-call board shows who understood; workers never see each other's mistakes; Ravi confirms from the board.
6. **Safety briefing.** Work at height: Murugan says three of the four points; the roll-call shows "3 of 4 points" and the harness point as not said yet. An extra check, not a guarantee.
7. **Montage.** Voice input and read-aloud (buttons shown, not pressed), Make this a task, Remind, site words (empty list in the demo room), insights (sample data), printable record.
8. **End card.** App Key `qd46yzrfq1obf` · Next.js on Vercel · Supabase · Nexconn Chat SDK · Team Kaching · Vaibhav Jangid. The "no pilot yet" statement is in the write-up and form summary, not on the end card.
